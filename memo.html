SUBJECT: Updated Assessment Dates and Training Schedule

This is to inform all trainees of the upcoming examination and assessment schedules for the various examining bodies. Please take note of the specific dates applicable to your category to ensure adequate preparation and timely attendance.

Candidate Category Assessment Start Date End Date
KNEC Candidates 13/03/2026 02/04/2026
TVET CDACC Candidates 16/03/2026 13/04/2026
TKNP Modular Candidates 30/03/2026 15/04/2026

Please be advised that regular training and class attendance for TVET CDACC and modular groups will continue until March 13 and March 27, 2026 respectively. It is mandatory for all TVET CDACC and Modular trainees to remain in session until these dates to finalize on portfolios and practical requirements.

Key Requirements for Candidates

  • Exam cards: All trainees must collect their exam cards from the Exams/Assessment office at least three days before their start date.
  • Fee Clearance: Only trainees that have cleared fee will be cleared to sit for the assessments.
  • Punctuality: Candidates must be at the examination rooms/workshops at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time.

We wish all our trainees success in their upcoming assessments. Let us maintain high levels of integrity during this examination season.
